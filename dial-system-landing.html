The four settings

Every situation calls for a different setting.

The framework isn't a mindset — it's a dial. Four settings, each with its own trigger conditions, and a way to move between them without losing your footing.

SETTING 01

Systems

See the whole board before you move. Map incentives, feedback loops, and second-order effects before reacting to the first-order problem.

SETTING 02

Range

Widen your repertoire of responses. The goal isn't one correct move — it's having more moves available than the situation expects.

SETTING 03

Patience

Hold position until timing is right. Disciplined patience is a skill, not an absence of urgency — it's urgency under control.

SETTING 04

Acceptance

Operate from a fixed sense of self. Every other setting depends on this one — you can't recalibrate a foundation that keeps moving.
